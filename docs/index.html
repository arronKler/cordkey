Capabilities

Built for real desk setups, not just one headset.

Every detail is engineered around daily voice dictation, multi-headset desks, and zero-friction macOS ergonomics.

🎧

Universal USB/HID (Consumer + Telephony) & One-Click Learn

Works out of the box with EarPods media buttons (0x0C) and dedicated Mic Mute / Hook Switch / Call Hold buttons (0x0B) on Jabra, Poly, Logitech, and HyperX USB headsets. Click Learn Button, press any button on your headset, and CordKey automatically binds both Vendor/Product ID and HID usage code.

🔒

Input Microphone Lock

Lock your preferred CoreAudio microphone by stable UID so macOS stops silently hijacking your default input whenever you replug USB-C EarPods.

⚡

Multi-Action: Tap + Double-Tap + Hold

Assign independent Short Tap (native Play/Pause or custom key), Double Tap, and Hold Delay (250 / 300 / 500 ms) actions on a single button.

🫧

Floating Status HUD & Safety Timeout

Optional non-activating frosted-glass pill visible over full-screen Spaces — shows elapsed time, lets you click to release, and pairs with 1m / 2m / 5m toggle auto-release.

🔄

Per-Headset Auto-Profiles

Remember separate mappings and trigger modes per headset VID:PID — plug in your office Jabra or travel EarPods and your profile switches automatically.

🧩

App-Aware Rules & Bluetooth Headset Mode

Auto-pause mapping in Music, IINA, or QuickTime so playback controls stay native, or override Play to mute shortcuts in Lark, Zoom, and Discord. Enable Bluetooth Compatibility Mode to use AirPods, Sony, or Bose headsets via Click Toggle when no USB headset is plugged in.

⌨️

True Left/Right Modifier Recording

Emits authentic IOKit device masks (NX_DEVICERALTKEYMASK, etc.) so Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, and VoiceInk accurately detect Right Option, Fn/Globe, and chords.

Hardware-Aware Engineering

Why EarPods go silent when you hold the center button.

Apple's inline remote shares one electrical wire with the microphone — physically holding the center button shorts the EarPods mic to ground. CordKey detects this exact setup and gives you two effortless fixes right inside the popover.

1. Clean-Audio Click Toggle (60 ms settle) Engages 60 ms after physical release so the mic is completely un-shorted with zero click pop, and releases instantly on the 2nd press.
2. One-Click Built-in Mic + Mic Lock Keep Hold-to-Hold feel by switching input to your MacBook Pro Mic and locking it — EarPods stays your audio output while your Mac mic captures clean speech.

Menu Bar App & CLI

Build in seconds. Zero dependencies.

Requires only macOS 13+ and Xcode Command Line Tools. Run as CordKey.app in your menu bar or invoke the same binary from your terminal or LaunchAgent.

zsh — cordkey
# 1. Install the native macOS menu bar app
$ git clone https://github.com/arronKler/cordkey.git && cd cordkey
$ make app-install && open /Applications/CordKey.app

# 2. Or run directly from CLI with full multi-action & Telephony support
$ ./bin/cordkey devices # list USB Consumer/Telephony headsets
$ ./bin/cordkey probe # inspect raw PAGE:USAGE codes live
$ ./bin/cordkey --play right_option --toggle --sound # EarPods clean-audio click toggle
$ ./bin/cordkey --play right_option --hold-ms 300 --tap space # dual-action: tap Space, hold Right Option
$ ./bin/cordkey --mic-mute right_option --vendor 0x0b0e # Jabra/Poly Telephony Mic Mute button
